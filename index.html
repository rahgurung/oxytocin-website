How it works

Built around your day

Open the app, do your rituals, feel close. Takes 3–5 minutes. Works even when life gets busy.

Open your daily rituals
Every day, Oxytocin surfaces a fresh set of activities tailored for the two of you. No decisions, no friction — just open and go.
Complete them together
Each of you completes activities on your own time — record a voice note, answer a question, plan a date. Your partner sees your responses when they check in.
Stay close, every day
Small, consistent moments add up. Oxytocin keeps growing — new rituals, new ways to connect. The habits you build here last.
